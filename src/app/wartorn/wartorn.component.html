OVERVIEW

As the sole UI/UX Designer on Wartorn, I was responsible for building the game’s interface from the ground up. This included designing an intuitive menu system and establishing the visual direction for all UI elements. My work shaped the player experience from early wireframes through to the final game, which has successfully launched into Early Access on Steam.

Squad Management System

A core objective of the game is to manage a collection of battle squads. These squads are recruits made of fantasy creatures that have different characteristics and abilities. Players will make decisions such as training and upgrading their squads in order to win the battles.


The Challenge

To design an interface that is organized with relevant information, easy to navigate, and visually engaging.


Personas

To identify the goals and needs of different player types, I created three persona profiles.


Task Flow

I created a flow to breakdown what actions players can take in this system.


Wireframes

Using Figma, I built wireframes to establish layout and content hierarchy.

The first design displays all the squads, so players can switch between squads easily. I grouped the abilities in one column and characteristics and stats in another with the Upgrade and Train action buttons at the lower bottom.


The second design separates the collection of squads onto another screen. This can act as an overview for players to see how each squad is progressing in relation to others. On the selected squad’s page, I moved the squad’s characteristics to the left and the abilities column to the right as it is clearer to read from left to right. To allow players to switch between squad views, there is a bar at the top and page arrows.

With these wireframes, I collaborated closely with the creative director and engineers to ensure the designs balanced player experience with technical feasibility.
